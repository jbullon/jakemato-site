How the hits are checked

Each booster uses MTGJSON's product-specific card sheets, including the printed finish and cards from additional sets where applicable. Secret Lair drops use individually listed product contents. Prices and original-print card art come from Scryfall. TCGplayer links point to the corresponding printing when available.

Prices are daily Scryfall USD market estimates, not real-time TCGplayer checkout prices. Only versions with a reported price strictly above $20 are shown. Missing prices, unpriced serialized variants, unverified products, and unknown Secret Lair bonus cards are not guessed. A missing source reference is flagged for the selected product.
